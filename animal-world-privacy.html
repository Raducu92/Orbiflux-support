Animal World: Color & Learn Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Using the game

Animal World does not require an account. The game stores drawings, progress and preferences in its local app storage so you can return to them. RDC Studio does not receive your saved drawings through the game. You can delete saved drawings in My Collection. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Unlock All purchase through the App Store. The game uses the purchase status provided by Apple to unlock content and restore access. RDC Studio does not receive your payment card details. Apple explains its handling of personal data in its privacy policy.

Support and deletion requests

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send. We use that information to reply and resolve the issue. We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve your request, handle follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of your support information. Please do not include information about a child in a support email.

Contact RDC Studio at rdc.studio.2d@gmail.com.
