Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Orbiflux.

Effective date: September 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Orbiflux is published by RDC Studio. For privacy or support questions, contact us at rdc.studio.2d@gmail.com .

2. Information stored by the game

Orbiflux does not require you to create an account or provide a name, username, or profile.

The game may store gameplay progress, settings, unlocked content, virtual currency balances, and preferences locally on your device so that your progress can be preserved between sessions.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Orbiflux uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising, including interstitial and rewarded ads.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, operating system settings, and Google's advertising configuration, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers or advertising identifiers, where permitted;
  • advertising data, such as ads shown and interactions with ads;
  • app interaction information;
  • diagnostic and performance information, including crash or performance data.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policies.

You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, Orbiflux may display a privacy or consent message before requesting advertising.

Your choices may affect whether advertising is personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted according to applicable law and platform settings.

Where available, you may review or change certain privacy choices using privacy options provided within the app, your device settings, or controls provided by the advertising provider.

5. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On supported Apple devices, Orbiflux may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing data that may be used for tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies.

If tracking permission is not granted, Orbiflux remains playable. Advertising may be less personalized or otherwise limited.

You can review or change tracking permissions at any time through the privacy settings on your Apple device.

6. Data sharing

RDC Studio does not sell personal information.

Information processed through advertising services may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising, subject to your consent choices, device settings, and applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress, settings, and preferences stored locally by Orbiflux generally remain on your device until they are cleared or the app is removed, subject to how your operating system handles backups and application data.

Data processed by Google or other third-party providers is handled according to those providers' own retention and deletion policies.

For questions about information handled directly by RDC Studio, contact rdc.studio.2d@gmail.com .

8. Children's privacy

Orbiflux does not require users to create accounts or submit names, profiles, or other account information directly to RDC Studio.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to RDC Studio, please contact us so that we can review the request.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled directly by Orbiflux. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services used by the app operate according to their own security practices and policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Orbiflux, its advertising configuration, third-party services, platform requirements, or applicable laws change.

When material changes are made, the effective date displayed at the top of this page will be updated.
