Glow Path: Draw & Roll

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Glow Path.

Effective date: September 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Glow Path: Draw & Roll is published by RDC Studio. For privacy or support questions, contact us at rdc.studio.2d@gmail.com.

2. Information stored by the game

Glow Path does not require you to create an account or provide a name, username, or profile. The game may store gameplay progress, settings, and preferences locally on your device so that your progress can be preserved between sessions.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Glow Path uses Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising, including interstitial and rewarded ads.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and the configuration of Google's advertising services, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • advertising data, such as ads shown or interactions with ads;
  • app interaction information;
  • diagnostic and performance information, including crash or performance data.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policies. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Consent and personalized advertising

Where required, Glow Path may display a consent message before requesting ads. Your choices may affect whether advertising is personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted by applicable law and platform settings.

You may be able to change certain privacy or consent choices through device settings or privacy options made available in the app or by the advertising provider.

5. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On supported Apple devices, Glow Path may ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing access to data used for tracking across apps or websites. If you decline, the app remains playable, although advertising may be less personalized.

You can review or change tracking permissions in your iOS privacy settings.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information processed through advertising services may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game progress and preferences remain on your device until they are cleared or the app is removed, subject to how your operating system handles backups and app data.

Data processed by Google or other advertising providers is handled according to those providers' retention and deletion policies. For questions about information handled directly by RDC Studio, contact rdc.studio.2d@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

Glow Path does not ask users to create accounts or submit profile information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to RDC Studio, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled directly by the game. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and third-party services operate under their own security practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, advertising configuration, platform requirements, or applicable laws change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
