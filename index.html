Common questions

The game is not playing sound

Check your device volume and sound settings, then fully close and reopen the game.

An ad did not load

Ads require an internet connection and may not always be available. Please try again later.

How do I reset local game data?

Glow Path stores game progress and preferences locally on your device. Removing the app may remove locally stored game data.
