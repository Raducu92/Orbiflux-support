Common questions

How do I restore Unlock All?

Open Shop from the home screen and follow the parental gate. On the Complete Collection screen, tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Where are my saved drawings?

After saving a drawing, return to the home screen and open My Collection.
